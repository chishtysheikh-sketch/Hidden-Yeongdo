Common questions

Before you come

Short, honest answers about the tours. Can't find yours? Ask us below.

  1. Tours are guided in English and Korean. Tell us which one you prefer when you book.

  2. You walk about 8 km during the day on coastal paths, stairs and pebble beaches, including a beginner-friendly hike to the 395 m summit of Bongnaesan. The longest parts are the 2-hour coastal walk and the hike of about 2¼ hours, with stops along the way. Comfortable trainers or hiking shoes are a must.

  3. Yes, children are welcome. On the 1-Day Tour, children aged 6–12 pay ₩32,500 (adults ₩57,000). The Half-day Sunset Walk is ₩20,000 per person.

  4. Anyone with a valid student ID pays ₩46,500 instead of ₩57,000. Please bring your student ID on the day.

  5. In heavy rain we skip the summit and add a café stop on the coast instead. If the weather is unsafe, we move your tour to another date or give you a full refund. See our booking terms.

  6. No, sorry. The route uses narrow coastal trails, stairs, a mountain path and a pebble beach, so it is not suitable for wheelchairs. If you have limited mobility, message us and we will tell you honestly which parts are possible.

  7. Yes. Lunch and dinner on the 1-Day Tour are seafood meals (a haenyeo seafood lunch and grilled clams), and we can arrange vegetarian, halal and seafood-free meals instead. Please tell us about your diet or allergies when you book.

  8. You can pay by bank transfer, cash or card. You don't pay when you send a booking request: after we confirm your date, we send you the payment details by email.

  9. At Nampo Station Exit 6 (Busan Metro Line 1). The 1-Day Tour starts at 09:30. Open the meeting point in Naver Map.

  10. If you need to cancel, please let us know at least 24 hours before the tour. If a tour cannot run because of the weather or too few guests, you get another date or a full refund. Full details are in our booking terms.

  11. No. We travel by local bus on Yeongdo, so please bring a T-money card with enough credit. The guide, seafood lunch, grilled-clam dinner and bottled water are included in the 1-Day Tour.

  12. Call 010-4329-4220 or send a KakaoTalk message to ID chishty . You can also email chishtysheikh@gmail.com.
