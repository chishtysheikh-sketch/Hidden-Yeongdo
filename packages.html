  1. Meet your guide and take a local bus across the bridge to Yeongdo. Bring a charged T-money card.

  2. Start at the tile mosaic wall and walk down to the sea. Follow the cliff path along pebble shores, with views of Namhang Bridge and ships waiting offshore, on a trail that was once a restricted military area.

  3. Seafood lunch prepared by haenyeo (women divers) at the end of the coastal walk.

  4. A guided, beginner-friendly hike on shaded pine trails to the 395 m summit. Hear the legend of the Yeongdo Grandmother, enjoy a 360° view of the harbour and city, then descend to the east side of the island.

  5. A 270 m pebble beach next to Taejongdae. Walk part of the gentle 3 km seaside promenade and listen to the pebbles rolling in the waves.

  6. Dinner at the grilled-clam tents by the beach (Jagalmadang) while the sun sets over the sea. Tour ends around 18:00; buses run back to Nampo.

Half-day Sunset Walk

Saturdays & Sundays · about 3 hours, ending at sunset · max 10 people · English / Korean

₩20,000 / person

Book this walk

Short on time? Meet at Nampo Station Exit 6 in the late afternoon, ride the local bus across the island and walk the seaside promenade to Gamji Beach for the sunset. We bring water and some snacks; grilled-clam dinner by the beach is optional and paid on the spot.

The start time follows the sunset, about 2½ hours before it, so it changes with the season. We confirm the exact time when we confirm your booking.

How booking works

  2. We confirm within 24 hours and send the payment details and meeting instructions. You can pay by bank transfer, cash or card.
  3. Meet your guide at Nampo Station Exit 6 (Line 1).
